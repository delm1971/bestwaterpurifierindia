1. Matching Purification Technology (RO, UV, UF) to Your Water Source

The primary criteria when choosing a home purifier is testing your input water's TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) level:

Groundwater / Borewell & Tankers (High TDS > 300 ppm) → Choose RO + UV + UF

Pros: Reverse Osmosis (RO) membranes effectively filter out heavy dissolved salts, heavy metals, pesticides, and nitrates. Combined UV and UF stages destroy remaining bacteria and micro-contaminants.
Cons: Generates reject wastewater and requires electrical power for the internal booster pump.

Municipal Supply / Soft Tap Water (Low TDS < 200–300 ppm) → Choose UV + UF

Pros: Preserves beneficial natural minerals (calcium and magnesium) while rendering biological contaminants harmless. Zero water wastage.
Cons: Does not lower dissolved mineral content or heavy salt taste.

Key Requirements Before Installing an Under-Counter Purifier

  • Cabinet Clearance: Verify that your lower sink cabinet has at least 1.5 feet of vertical and horizontal clearance free from drain pipes.
  • Power & Plumbing Access: Ensure a 5A electrical socket and a cold-water supply valve are accessible under the counter.
  • Faucet Hole: Standard installations require a ~28mm hole drilled into your sink rim or granite countertop for mounting the dedicated dispenser tap.
