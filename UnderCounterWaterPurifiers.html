Which Purification Type Works Best in Under-Counter Formats?

RO (Reverse Osmosis) Systems — Essential for High TDS Water (> 300 ppm)

If your apartment receives deep borewell water or municipal tanker water with elevated hardness, heavy metals, or dissolved solids, an Under-Counter RO system is essential. It strips away dissolved impurities while utilizing an air-pressurized hydrostatic tank to drive water up through the countertop faucet at high speed.

UV & UF Systems — Ideal for Soft Municipal Supply (< 200–300 ppm)

For city supply with low dissolved minerals, compact Under-Counter UV + UF purifiers eliminate viruses and bacteria without generating reject wastewater. These direct-flow systems take up minimal cabinet volume beneath the sink because they do not require a separate storage tank.

3 Critical Factors to Evaluate Before Purchasing

  • Under-Sink Installation Space: Measure your under-sink cabinet volume carefully. Standard under-counter RO units with hydrostatic tanks require approximately 1.5 feet of vertical height, 1.5 feet of width, and 1.5 feet of depth. Ensure clear space around plumbing pipes and garbage disposals.
  • Water Dispensing Flow Rate & Pressure: Under-counter purifiers rely on an internal booster pump and pressurized tank to push water up to the sink tap. Confirm that the model includes an active pressure pump for consistent water flow.
  • Maintenance Access & Filter Replacements: Modern under-counter systems feature quick-twist or front-facing filter cartridges. Ensure your sink cabinet offers enough room for service technicians to open the unit and replace filters annually without removing the entire system.
